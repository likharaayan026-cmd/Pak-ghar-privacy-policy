PakGhar Privacy Policy

Ghar jaisa khana • Simple & Privacy-Friendly

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PakGhar. This Privacy Policy explains how PakGhar handles information when you use our application.

PakGhar is a recipe and cooking assistance application that allows users to enter ingredients, generate recipe suggestions, save favorite recipes, view recipe history, select cooking preferences, and share recipes with others.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only the information reasonably required to operate and improve the application.

2. Information We Collect

PakGhar does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or email address to use the core recipe features.

Information entered by the user

When using the application, you may enter information such as:

  • Ingredients or food items
  • Number of people/servings
  • Cooking goals or preferences
  • Recipe search and filter preferences

Information stored on your device

PakGhar uses browser/device local storage to remember certain application preferences and content, including:

  • Favorite recipes
  • Recipe history
  • Selected language preference
  • Selected serving size
Important: These items are stored locally on your device and are not intended to identify you personally.

3. Firebase & Usage Statistics

PakGhar uses Firebase services for limited application statistics and technical functionality.

The application may record aggregate statistics such as the total number of recipe generations and the total number of recipes generated.

These statistics are used to understand general application usage and improve the application experience.

PakGhar does not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, or account profile information to Firebase because the application does not require an account for its core features.

4. Recipe & Cooking Information

Ingredients and cooking preferences entered into PakGhar are used to provide recipe suggestions and related application features.

Recipe information may include ingredients, preparation steps, cooking time, serving information, nutritional estimates, difficulty and other recipe-related details.

Nutrition Notice: Nutritional information displayed by the application may be approximate and should not be considered medical or professional dietary advice.

5. WhatsApp & External Sharing

PakGhar provides an option to share recipes through WhatsApp or other supported sharing applications on your device.

When you choose to share content, the information is transferred according to the privacy policy and terms of the external service you choose to use.

PakGhar does not control how an external service processes information after you choose to share it.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party technical services and libraries required for application functionality, including Firebase, web fonts, and icon libraries.

Third-party services may process technical information such as network requests, device/browser information, or other information required to provide their services.

Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

7. Advertising

At the time of this Privacy Policy, PakGhar does not describe a personalized advertising profile or require users to provide personal information for advertising purposes.

If advertising or advertising-related services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid entering sensitive, confidential, financial, medical, or other highly personal information into recipe inputs.

9. Children's Privacy

PakGhar is a general-purpose cooking and recipe application. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through account registration because the application does not require an account for its core features.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Data Deletion

Because favorites, history, language preferences, and serving preferences are stored locally on your device, you can generally remove this locally stored information by clearing the application's data or local storage, or by uninstalling the application.

Aggregate application statistics are not tied to your personal identity through the core features described in this policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, technology, legal requirements, or data practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
